## D2 — mixed5xx / hard-error retry limitation

准确位置：[transient_response](/private/tmp/ucsd-lab-summaries-20261016/data/local-lab-summaries/network-adapter-sources/collect_ucsd_coverage-edaacebf859c73b14b76b41673b4c30089b65c987c41baf2d4294015bae9d439.py:91)，尤其第95–99行。
